How it works

  1. Step 1

    Post what you need

    Ask for supplies, a lab tour, or a speaker. Paste a protocol and Quippe works out the supply list and amounts for your group size.

  2. Step 2

    Nearby companies say yes

    A liaison at each company sees the asks that fit their surplus and schedule, and accepts with one click. High school asks go to your advisor first.

  3. Step 3

    Keep the relationship

    Every donation, tour, and talk goes on your club's timeline. When officers graduate, Quippe writes the handoff for next year's board.

For clubs

Built for groups that run on a small budget and a lot of emails: STEM clubs, affinity groups like SACNAS and SHPE chapters, and undergrad research labs.

  • One place to ask every company near you, instead of cold emailing each one
  • Supply lists sized to your group, with grocery and pharmacy swaps for what's left

Why we built this

We ran a STEM club for underrepresented students with almost no budget. Every workshop meant cold emailing companies for pipette tips and agarose, and hoping someone wrote back.

The companies we did reach wanted to help. They just had no easy way to find us. Quippe is that way.
