Protocol, adapted for a classroom

Groups of 5. No centrifuge needed.

  1. Mash 2 strawberries in a zip bag with 10 mL of extraction buffer (soap, salt, water) for 2 minutes.
  2. Filter through a coffee filter into a tube and keep about 2 mL.
  3. Tilt the tube and slowly add 4 mL of cold isopropyl alcohol down the side.
  4. Wait 3 minutes. Spool the white DNA strands with a transfer pipette.
  5. Load 10 µL of each group's sample with loading dye next to the ladder.
  6. Run the 1% gel at 100 V for 30 minutes, then compare bands as a class.
